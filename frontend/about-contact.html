Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about flying with SkyWings.

How early can I check in online for my flight?

▼
Online check-in opens 24 hours prior to scheduled flight departure and closes 60 minutes before departure. You can select your seats, confirm passenger details, and instantly download or save your official electronic boarding pass.

What is the baggage allowance on international flights?

▼
Economy Class includes 1x 23kg checked bag + 1x 7kg carry-on. Business Class includes 2x 32kg checked bags + 2x 10kg carry-on. First Class includes 3x 32kg checked bags with priority baggage tags.

Can I change or cancel my booking online?

▼
Yes. You can manage your flights directly under "My Bookings". Confirmed and held bookings can be cancelled with instantaneous refund processing and release of seat holds.

How do I present my boarding pass at the airport?

▼
You can present either the downloaded PDF boarding pass or the saved mobile PNG pass image on your smartphone at security checkpoints and the departure gate. Physical printing is optional.
